Politika privatnosti — srpski

1. Ukratko

Pčelarnik je digitalni pčelarski dnevnik. Aplikacija ne zahteva korisnički nalog i nema sopstveni server za evidenciju, sinhronizaciju u oblak, reklame ni analitiku. Evidencija i prilozi koje unesete čuvaju se prvenstveno u privatnom prostoru aplikacije na vašem telefonu.

Aplikacija ne deklariše Android dozvole INTERNET i ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. Ipak, QR skener koristi Google Play usluge / ML Kit Code Scanner. Google Play usluge su zasebna sistemska komponenta i mogu imati sopstvene zahteve za preuzimanje ili obradu koje Pčelarnik ne kontroliše. Ova politika zato ne tvrdi da Google, Android, druge aplikacije koje izaberete ili mrežni operator nikada ne obrađuju podatke.

2. Podaci koje Pčelarnik čuva

U zavisnosti od funkcija koje koristite, aplikacija može lokalno čuvati:

  • nazive i opise pčelinjaka, tekst lokacije i opcione geografske koordinate;
  • oznake i detalje košnica, te evidenciju pregleda, tretmana, prihrane, vrcanja, događaja matica, premeštanja i zadataka;
  • fotografije koje dodate uz pregled (do tri po pregledu); aplikacija ih umanjuje i ponovo kodira kao JPEG, pa sačuvana kopija ne zadržava EXIF/GPS metapodatke originala;
  • jednu glasovnu belešku do 60 sekundi po pregledu, ako je snimite; aplikacija je ne pretvara u tekst;
  • podešavanja kao što su jezik, lokalno ime profila, izgled kartica i preference podsetnika.

Korisnik može napraviti ZIP rezervnu kopiju koja uključuje evidenciju i priloge. ZIP nije šifrovan; zaštitite ga kao poverljiv dokument.

3. Dozvole i funkcije uređaja

Aplikacija deklariše sledeće Android dozvole: ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, RECORD_AUDIO, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED i WAKE_LOCK, kao i internu dozvolu za sopstveni prijemnik. Ne deklariše INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE ni CAMERA. Android automatsko cloud rezervno kopiranje aplikacije je isključeno (allowBackup=false). Dozvole se traže tek kada koristite funkciju koja ih zahteva, a ne pri prvom pokretanju.

  • Lokacija (precizna ili približna): traži se kada dodirnete da zabeležite trenutnu lokaciju pčelinjaka. Lokacija se koristi u prvom planu i nema praćenja kretanja u pozadini. Koordinate se čuvaju lokalno.
  • Mikrofon: RECORD_AUDIO se traži kada pokrenete snimanje glasovne beleške. Snimak se čuva lokalno uz evidenciju i traje do 60 sekundi.
  • Obaveštenja: POST_NOTIFICATIONS se traži kada uključite podsetnik zadatka. Podsetnici se zakazuju lokalno na telefonu (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED i WAKE_LOCK služe da se zakazani podsetnici obnove posle restarta). Tačno vreme isporuke nije zagarantovano; obaveštenje može da zakasni nekoliko minuta.
  • Kamera: „Uslikaj“ otvara zasebnu sistemsku aplikaciju kamere i traži da fotografiju upiše u privatni fajl Pčelarnika. Pčelarnik ne traži dozvolu CAMERA i ne pristupa direktno senzoru. Izabrana kamera može imati sopstvenu obradu.
  • Izbor i čuvanje fajlova: za fotografije, uvoz/izvoz kopije i izveštaje koristi se sistemski birač. Aplikacija dobija samo fajl koji izaberete za konkretnu radnju; ne traži pristup celoj galeriji.
  • QR skeniranje: skeniranje poziva Google Play usluge / ML Kit Code Scanner. Pčelarnik dobija samo dekodirani tekst QR koda i ne čuva sliku skeniranja. Dostupnost i eventualno preuzimanje sistemskog modula zavise od Google Play usluga i uređaja.

Dozvole možete odbiti ili promeniti u Android podešavanjima. Odbijanje isključuje samo povezanu funkciju.

4. Prosleđivanje podataka i druge aplikacije

Pčelarnik ne šalje evidenciju na sopstveni server. To ne znači da se podaci nikada ne prosleđuju van aplikacije: radnje koje sami pokrenete i sistemske komponente mogu ih preneti ili obraditi.

  • Kada izaberete „Otvori u aplikaciji za mape“, koordinate, a po potrebi i oznaka lokacije, prosleđuju se aplikaciji koju izaberete za tu radnju. Dalju obradu uređuje pružalac te aplikacije.
  • Sistemski birač omogućava da izaberete fajl za čitanje ili odredište za čuvanje; pristup zavisi od verzije Androida i izabranog provajdera.
  • ZIP kopiju čuvate ili delite sami. Ona može sadržati nazive, koordinate, zapise, fotografije i snimke; pristup i obrada na odredištu nisu pod kontrolom Pčelarnika. Aplikacija nema ugrađeno dugme za direktno deljenje.
  • QR skener pružaju Google Play usluge / ML Kit, a fotografiju snima sistemska kamera; njihovu obradu uređuju njihovi pružaoci.

QR kod koji Pčelarnik napravi sadrži samo interni identifikator košnice, a ne njenu oznaku, naziv pčelinjaka ni koordinate.

5. Nalozi, reklame i analitika

Pčelarnik nema korisničke naloge, sopstveni backend, sinhronizaciju u oblak, reklame ni analitiku. Sistemske komponente i druge aplikacije koje koristite mogu imati sopstvenu mrežnu obradu.

6. Čuvanje i brisanje

Arhiviranje košnice ili pčelinjaka čuva istoriju i nije brisanje. Brisanjem podataka aplikacije u Android podešavanjima ili deinstalacijom uklanjaju se lokalni podaci aplikacije. ZIP kopije sačuvane van aplikacije morate obrisati zasebno. Razvijač nema serversku kopiju vaše evidencije, pa ne može daljinski da je obriše ni da je vrati.

7. Deca

8. Bezbednost

Privacy Policy — English

Effective date: 14.10.2026.
Developer display name: Pcelarnik Studio (personal Google Play developer account)

1. In brief

Pčelarnik is a digital beekeeping journal. The app does not require a user account and has no server of its own for journal data, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics. Information you enter is stored primarily in the app's private storage on your phone.

The app does not declare the Android permissions INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. QR scanning nevertheless uses Google Play services / ML Kit Code Scanner. Google Play services is a separate system component and may have its own download or processing requirements that Pčelarnik does not control. This policy therefore does not claim that Google, Android, other apps you choose or a mobile carrier never process data.

2. Information Pčelarnik stores

Depending on the features you use, the app may store locally:

  • apiary names and descriptions, location text and optional geographic coordinates;
  • hive labels and details, and records of inspections, treatments, feeding, harvests, queen events, moves and tasks;
  • photos you add to an inspection (up to three per inspection); the app resizes and re-encodes them as JPEG, so the saved copy does not retain the original's EXIF/GPS metadata;
  • one voice note of up to 60 seconds per inspection, if you record it; the app does not transcribe it;
  • settings such as language, local profile name, card appearance and reminder preferences.

You can create a ZIP backup that includes your records and attachments. The ZIP is not encrypted; protect it like a confidential document.

3. Permissions and device features

The app declares these Android permissions: ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, RECORD_AUDIO, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and WAKE_LOCK, plus an internal permission for its own receiver. It does not declare INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE or CAMERA. Android automatic cloud backup of the app is disabled (allowBackup=false). Permissions are requested only when you use the feature that needs them, not on first launch.

  • Location (precise or approximate): requested when you tap to record an apiary's current location. It is used in the foreground; there is no background tracking. Coordinates are stored locally.
  • Microphone: RECORD_AUDIO is requested when you start recording a voice note. The recording is stored locally with the record and lasts up to 60 seconds.
  • Notifications: POST_NOTIFICATIONS is requested when you turn on a task reminder. Reminders are scheduled locally on the phone (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and WAKE_LOCK allow scheduled reminders to be restored after a restart). Exact delivery time is not guaranteed; a notification may be a few minutes late.
  • Camera: “Take photo” opens a separate system camera app and asks it to write the photo to a private Pčelarnik file. Pčelarnik does not request the CAMERA permission and does not access the sensor directly. The camera app may have its own processing.
  • File selection and saving: a system picker is used for photos, backup import/export and reports. The app receives only the file you choose for a specific action; it does not request access to your whole gallery.
  • QR scanning: scanning calls Google Play services / ML Kit Code Scanner. Pčelarnik receives only the decoded QR text and does not store the scan image. Availability and any download of the system module depend on Google Play services and your device.

You can deny or change permissions in Android settings. Denying a permission disables only the related feature.

4. Data transfers and other apps

Pčelarnik does not send journal data to a server of its own. This does not mean data never leaves the app: actions you start and system components may transfer or process it.

  • When you choose “Open in a maps app,” the coordinates and, where applicable, the location label are passed to the app you select for that action. That app's provider governs further processing.
  • The system picker lets you choose a file to read or a destination to save to; access depends on your Android version and the selected provider.
  • You store or share a ZIP backup yourself. It may contain names, coordinates, records, photos and recordings; access and processing at the destination are outside Pčelarnik's control. The app has no built-in direct share button.
  • The QR scanner is provided by Google Play services / ML Kit and photos are captured by the system camera; their providers govern their processing.

A QR code generated by Pčelarnik contains only an internal hive identifier, not its label, apiary name or coordinates.

5. Accounts, advertising and analytics

Pčelarnik has no user accounts, backend of its own, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics. System components and other apps you use may have their own network processing.

6. Retention and deletion

Journal data and attachments are stored in the app's private storage. Exporting creates a copy at the destination you choose and does not itself delete data in the app. Archiving a hive or apiary preserves its history and is not deletion. Clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its local data. ZIP backups saved outside the app must be deleted separately. The developer has no server copy of your records, and therefore cannot remotely delete or restore them.

7. Children

Pčelarnik is a tool for beekeepers and is not designed for children. The app does not ask for a date of birth and has no user accounts.

8. Security

The app keeps its records in Android app-private storage; this is not a promise that data on the device is encrypted. ZIP backups are not encrypted. Protect your device and backups, especially if they contain precise coordinates or business data. Pčelarnik provides no cloud synchronization or cloud backup.
